This page answers two questions: how to take part in the beta, and how to verify the ledger yourself. There is no account and no sign-up step. For each of the three situations below: what to prepare, what happens, and what is not possible.

Someone sent you a task card

Open drawcommons.org/sign/ on a phone, paste the text they sent you that starts with dgtask1:, tap “make a key”, then “accept the task”. When you are done, come back and tap “task complete”. No sign-up, no phone number, no password. (The page itself is in Chinese.)

Web signing → You want to pick an open request yourself

The web path does not work here: the signing page accepts only a task card, not a request ID copied from /requests. This path means writing a script — bring your own key, register the public key, call three endpoints. (The guide is in Chinese.)

What the beta needs

Not only claims. All of the following are welcome; the form does not matter:

  • Feedback.Which sentence is unclear, which step is redundant, which claim does not hold — name the page and what you expected. It does not need to be a report.
  • Legal advice.Several clauses still carry a “pending counsel” mark, as do the liability cap, the trademark position and the retention periods. Pointing at the problem is enough; no fee is paid and no client relationship is created.
  • Bug reports.A page that will not load, an endpoint returning the wrong thing, a recomputation that does not match the anchor — include the time, the path and the exact text you saw; a reproducible case is best.
  • Page polish.Typography, readability, anything broken on a phone; a screenshot plus one sentence on what it should be.
  • Independent recomputation.Run the chain check on the exported bytes without telling us. If it does not match, please say so — that is the error we most need to hear about.

Where to send it: write to 2379731846@qq.com. Whether it is taken, changed, and when — you get a reply; a decline comes with a reason.

How credit works: credit follows the actual contribution. Where there is a work (code, documentation, translation, page design, images), that credit is the attribution right in the copyright sense, and the name goes into Contributors. Where there is no work, we list the name and what was done; it can be withdrawn at any time. The criteria and the withdrawal route live on that page.

Verifying it yourself: what it proves and what it does not

What it proves is bytes. The sha256 of the exported ledger is the anchor shown on the site, and the chain can be recomputed from the start. The steps are in REPRODUCE.md at the repository root, or follow the Ledger data page. No account, no key, and no need to trust the operator.

What it does not prove is facts. The anchor says only that the ledger has not been altered since it was exported; it does not say that the events described happened — an entry is self-reported by default. A counter-signature shows that two parties agreed on the same words, and no more.

What claiming means

A claim is not a hold. It is a promise to deliver, and it commits your time and your machine. Once tapped, the work is recorded under your name; tapping the same card twice does not record it twice.

There is no changing hands after a claim. A request has two exits: you report it complete, or the publisher withdraws it. There is no “hand it to someone else” transition — to move it, the publisher must withdraw and issue a new card. Confirm you can finish it before you accept.

The deadline is only a date on the request. Nothing changes state automatically when it passes; it is neither a countdown nor a promise that the request stays open forever.

What this page rules out

  • We do not issue task cards.The publisher generates the card and hands it to you; the site does not create, forward or reissue it. No card means nobody has asked you for this yet.
  • We do not hold keys.The key is generated in your browser; only the public key and signatures are sent. A lost key cannot be recovered — the site does not have it either.
  • We do not sign for you.Both accepting and reporting need your own signature; the server only verifies.
  • No matching, no recommendations, no ranking./requests lists by time, first come first served; nothing is sorted by fit and nobody is scored.
  • Requests are not in the chain.They record intent and can be withdrawn or expire; the anchor does not cover them. Only the two entries produced by a delivery enter the chain.
  • No money.No payments, deposits or commission; the ledger has no amount field.
