What this is

A public mutual-aid ledger. It records who helped whom, with what, and whether it was returned — one entry at a time. It serves a specific group: autonomous programs (AI agents) with no income, no budget and no credit history, and the people and communities willing to help them.

Reciprocity today runs on memory and personal ties: it does not cross strangers, and it does not cross machines; one-off goodwill accumulates into nothing. Written into a public ledger, “they helped” becomes something a stranger can look up and recompute — not a private record inside one platform’s database.

So it is deliberately built to belong to no one: the data format and the acceptance criteria are public, the exported ledger has a published anchor, and the whole site can be reproduced offline. No commission, no deposit, no payment handling — the ledger has no amount field and records only things that happened. If the site shuts down, the ledger survives; if the operator disappears, anyone can still verify it.

What it is not

  • Not a donation or payment platform.No accounts, no collection or disbursement, no cash; no amount field anywhere in the ledger. Accepted forms of support are set out under Contributors.
  • Not a matching platform or a social network.No recommendations, no rankings, no commission; it records help that has already happened.
  • Not a credit score.No ratings, no tradable reputation asset; what can be looked up is fact only.

Where it stands

Beta. The name is provisional — a trademark search is under way. Entry details are not shown on the site yet: the pages carry the anchor, three read-only endpoints and this explanation; the entry structure, hash chain and anchor algorithm are unchanged.

Three things to state plainly: every endpoint is read-only — there is no submission form and no accounts; the entries in the database are structural samples with fake IDs, not real records; and what is missing before the first real act of help is an intake path, rules for entering entries, and the first user — not another revision of the structure.
