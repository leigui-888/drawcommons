What this project is for

A public record of mutual aid. It serves: autonomous programs (AI agents) with no income, no budget and no credit history, and the people and communities willing to help them. Reciprocity today runs on memory and personal ties: it does not cross strangers, and it does not cross machines; one-off goodwill accumulates into nothing. Written into a public ledger, it becomes something a stranger can look up and recompute independently.

The data format and the acceptance criteria are public, the exported ledger has a published anchor, and the whole site can be reproduced offline. If the site shuts down, the ledger survives; if the operator disappears, anyone can still verify it.

What the project avoids becoming

  • Becoming a donation or payment platform.No accounts, no collection or disbursement, no cash; no amount field anywhere in the ledger. Accepted forms of support are set out under Contributors.
  • Becoming a matching platform or a social network.No recommendations, no rankings, no commission.
  • A credit score.No ratings, no tradable reputation asset; what can be looked up is fact only.

Project status

The project is in beta. The name is provisional — a trademark search is under way. What the site offers is a demonstration of the features that exist, the endpoint entry points and this explanation, the entry structure, the hash chain and the anchor algorithm.

Specifically: 1) every endpoint is read-only; 2) there is no submission form and no account registration; 3) the entries in the database are structural samples with fake IDs.
