What this number is, and is not

It is not the non-member benefit rate. The read endpoints are anonymous and the logs carry no identity field at all, so “is this reader a member?” cannot be decided — see the third section below for why.

It is an upper bound: a hand-run curl with a different user agent lands in it too. Read it as a trend, not as an achievement.

Three things we cannot measure

Non-member benefit rate · ratio

The read endpoints are anonymous; logs hold only IP and user agent. Computing it needs an optional identity on the read side — a protocol-level change that has to go through the notice period.

Independent deployments / integrations

No public registry, no reporting endpoint, and no telemetry. Reporting behind people’s backs to feed a metric would be worse than not having the number.

Share surviving 90 days

A survival rate needs a list of instances first; without the item above there is no item here.

What this page does not do

No rankings, no scores, no treating a number as an achievement. Nor do we pass our own self-tests off as evidence of outside use — that is why probes and crawlers are listed apart instead of blended into the main figure.
